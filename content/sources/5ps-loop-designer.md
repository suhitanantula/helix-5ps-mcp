# Design a 5Ps Loop

Turn a real strategic challenge into a living loop: 〰 perceive the system, ▲ perform with intent, ● build a portfolio, ●〰 choose what matters, and ▲● build learning architecture.

## Perceive

〰 Cultural sensing · Sensing intelligence

Begin not with answers, but with attention. Perceive combines lived experience, weak signals, prospective sensemaking, and AI pattern recognition to understand what truly matters before solution-building begins.

Who is affected?
What signals are you sensing?
What assumptions need testing?
Where can AI extend perception?

## Perform

▲ Intentional execution · Execution intelligence

Insight without action is delay. Perform converts strategic clarity into concrete moves, using AI to scale execution while preserving human intent, values, and strategic boundaries.

What concrete action should happen first?
What intent must be preserved?
Which repeated decisions could be systematised?
What needs human judgement?

## Portfolio

● Adaptive resilience · Option intelligence

Don't seek one perfect strategy. Build an option system that gets stronger from uncertainty — safe bets, bold experiments, and learning loops that turn volatility into capability.

What are the reliable moves?
What are the bold experiments?
How will resources flow?
How will uncertainty strengthen the system?

## Pause / Promote

●〰 Strategic judgement · Discernment intelligence

In the AI age, the problem is not too few options — it is too many. Pause/Promote designs filtering architectures that combine AI pattern recognition with human values, context, and judgement.

What criteria decide what matters?
What should be promoted?
What should be paused or killed?
What judgement risks must you guard against?

## Progress

▲● Systematic learning · Learning intelligence

Progress is not making one thing better. It is building systems that keep getting better — learning architecture that captures outcomes, synthesises meaning, and transfers capability across the organisation.

What feedback will the loop capture?
Who turns data into meaning?
How will learning transfer?
What triggers the next Perceive cycle?
