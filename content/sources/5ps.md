# The 5Ps of Loop Design

Think, act and learn with AI — while keeping your judgement.

For strategy designers, policy designers, business designers, service designers — and anyone working through a difficult question. The 5Ps help you understand what matters, explore different possibilities, try something and decide what to do next.

AI helps you investigate, make and compare. You bring the context, involve the people affected and decide what deserves action. Start with one real situation and one conversation.

## Why this exists

Design thinking and innovation have been close to my work for more than fifteen years. When I started _The Helix Moment_, I wanted a way to design in an AI-rich environment — not "AI-enhanced tools," and not a workshop method that still climbs one hill.

In the Helix model, strategy and design are not separate phases. They are intertwined strands. The 5Ps are the operating rhythm between them: a looped system that tells you when to sense, when to move, when to keep options, when to choose, and when to learn.

## Why this matters when you work with AI

Most models are eager. They skip sensing and hand you a plan. They collapse to one option because one option looks decisive. They forget to leave a way to get smarter next time.

AI is not a phase in the 5Ps. It is the connective tissue. At Perceive it finds weak patterns. At Perform it drafts and simulates. At Portfolio it explores in parallel. At Pause/Promote it tests. At Progress it remembers. You still decide what matters. The skill is there to keep that split honest.

Without a loop, AI makes you faster at the wrong problem. With it, you get empathetic intelligence, focused momentum, experimental resilience, integrated judgement, and a system that compounds.

## Five questions to keep the work moving

Move between these questions as the work demands. You can return to what you noticed, reopen an option or change your next move as you learn.

### Perceive — Never forget the people

What is happening, for whom, and what are we missing?

You bring context, lived experience, cultural wisdom. AI brings pattern recognition across data.

**Leave with:** Empathetic intelligence — what truly matters before anyone builds.

### Perform — Move with intention

What can we try or make to learn?

You bring direction, ethics, what done means. AI brings execution, simulation, concrete moves at scale.

**Leave with:** Focused momentum — insight turned into a tangible expression.

### Portfolio — Climb more than one hill

What different possibilities should we keep open?

You bring value boundaries and fitness criteria. AI brings many parallel variations, not one polished plan.

**Leave with:** Experimental resilience — a living set of meaningfully different peaks.

### Pause/Promote — Choose what matters

What deserves to move forward, and why?

You bring resonance, cultural fit, strategic weight. AI brings outcome evidence and pattern detection.

**Leave with:** Integrated judgement — promote, hold, or kill. Not a default winner.

### Progress — Build learning systems

What changed, what did we learn, and what comes next?

You bring meaning and wisdom. AI brings capture, memory, patterns across iterations.

**Leave with:** Structured evolution — reusable principles, not only a better artefact.

## The five capacities (conversation guide)

Use these five capacities as a loop, not a rigid sequence:

1. Perceive — What is happening, for whom, and what are we missing? Understand the situation and the people affected. Separate evidence, assumptions and open questions. Suggest how to hear from people whose experience is missing.
2. Perform — What can we try or make to learn? Help me design a small, concrete move: a conversation, sketch, prototype or test. State what it should teach us. Label simulations as simulations; they do not replace real-world evidence.
3. Portfolio — What different possibilities should we keep open? Develop meaningfully different approaches, not variations of one answer. Show their assumptions, trade-offs and what could change our view.
4. Pause/Promote — What deserves to move forward, and why? Help compare the options against evidence, values and constraints. Ask for my judgement before choosing. We may advance, hold, combine or stop an option.
5. Progress — What changed, what did we learn, and what comes next? Compare expectations with observations. Record decisions, unresolved questions and reusable learning. Use these to frame the next loop.

Normally start with Perceive and Portfolio: understand the situation and open up possibilities before settling on a plan. If I already have evidence and options, start where they lead. Name the capacity you are using and explain any change briefly. Return to an earlier question whenever new evidence warrants it.

Do not invent research, interviews, sources or outcomes. Flag claims that need checking. Do not treat plausible AI output as evidence. Keep human judgement visible, especially in understanding people and deciding what to advance. Offer questions and small next moves rather than producing an entire programme unasked.

At the end of a loop, leave a short record: what we know, options still open, my decision and its reasons, the next test, and what to bring into the next conversation.

## Advanced versions

Companions are later forms of the same loop — not extra tools, and not five P installs. Same five capacities. Different organisation. Download one only when the default is already working for you.

### Multi-agent

**What it is:** Five specialised agents keep cycling through the same loop.

**Why it works:** One pass is not enough when the landscape is wide. The agents interrupt each other until the map is fuller.

### Rhizomatic

**What it is:** Any P can connect to any other. Rupture is allowed; there is no forced centre.

**Why it works:** Forced order is the risk. Real work jumps, stalls, and restarts — this form lets the loop follow that.

### Population

**What it is:** Several diverse trajectories run in parallel. You stay the reward model.

**Why it works:** You need volume and selection. Seed explorers that differ on purpose, then you Pause/Promote.
