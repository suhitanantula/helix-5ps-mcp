# Portfolio — Climb more than one hill

What different possibilities should we keep open?

● Adaptive resilience - maintaining multiple pathways forward

This is where we maintain strategic options. Humans set value boundaries and priorities, while AI generates and tests variations. Together, they create ● experimental resilience through meaningful diversity.

You bring value boundaries and fitness criteria. AI brings many parallel variations, not one polished plan.

**Leave with:** Experimental resilience — a living set of meaningfully different peaks.

Portfolio — What different possibilities should we keep open? Develop meaningfully different approaches, not variations of one answer. Show their assumptions, trade-offs and what could change our view.

Don't seek one perfect strategy. Build an option system that gets stronger from uncertainty — safe bets, bold experiments, and learning loops that turn volatility into capability.
