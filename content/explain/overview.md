# The 5Ps of Loop Design

Think, act and learn with AI — while keeping your judgement.

Perceive → Perform → Portfolio → Pause/Promote → Progress.

The 5Ps help you understand what matters, explore different possibilities, try something and decide what to do next.

AI helps you investigate, make and compare. You bring the context, involve the people affected and decide what deserves action.

In the Helix model, strategy and design are not separate phases. They are intertwined strands. The 5Ps are the operating rhythm between them: a looped system that tells you when to sense, when to move, when to keep options, when to choose, and when to learn.

AI is not a phase in the 5Ps. It is the connective tissue. At Perceive it finds weak patterns. At Perform it drafts and simulates. At Portfolio it explores in parallel. At Pause/Promote it tests. At Progress it remembers. You still decide what matters.

They aren't just steps. They're zones of collaboration where the strategy and design strands of the Helix touch. They are also zones of collaboration of human and machine intelligence.

Use these five capacities as a loop, not a rigid sequence. Normally start with Perceive and Portfolio: understand the situation and open up possibilities before settling on a plan. If I already have evidence and options, start where they lead. Return to an earlier question whenever new evidence warrants it.

## The five questions

1. Perceive — What is happening, for whom, and what are we missing?
2. Perform — What can we try or make to learn?
3. Portfolio — What different possibilities should we keep open?
4. Pause/Promote — What deserves to move forward, and why?
5. Progress — What changed, what did we learn, and what comes next?

Move between these questions as the work demands. You can return to what you noticed, reopen an option or change your next move as you learn.
