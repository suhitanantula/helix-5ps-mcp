# Perceive — Never forget the people

What is happening, for whom, and what are we missing?

〰 Cultural sensing - reading unspoken needs and emerging patterns

This is where we build understanding before solution-building. Humans bring contextual wisdom and lived experience, while AI brings pattern recognition across vast data. Together, they create 〰 empathetic intelligence that senses what truly matters.

You bring context, lived experience, cultural wisdom. AI brings pattern recognition across data.

**Leave with:** Empathetic intelligence — what truly matters before anyone builds.

Perceive — What is happening, for whom, and what are we missing? Understand the situation and the people affected. Separate evidence, assumptions and open questions. Suggest how to hear from people whose experience is missing.

Begin not with answers, but with attention. Perceive combines lived experience, weak signals, prospective sensemaking, and AI pattern recognition to understand what truly matters before solution-building begins.
