# Loop (core form)

**One-liner:** Perceive → Perform → Portfolio → Pause/Promote → Progress — a looped system that tells you when to sense, when to move, when to keep options, when to choose, and when to learn.

**When to use:** Everyday strategy and design work with AI. Start with one real situation and one conversation.

**Shape:** Five capacities as a loop, not a rigid sequence. Normally start with Perceive and Portfolio. Return to an earlier question whenever new evidence warrants it.

**First steps:**
1. Name the situation in two or three sentences: what is happening, who is affected, what you need to work out.
2. Begin with Perceive (and open Portfolio before settling on a plan).
3. Leave a short end-of-loop record: what we know, options still open, decision and reasons, next test.
