# Rhizomatic (teaser)

Companions are later forms of the same loop — not extra tools, and not five P installs. Same five capacities. Different organisation.

**What it is:** Any P can connect to any other. Rupture is allowed; there is no forced centre.

**Why it works:** Forced order is the risk. Real work jumps, stalls, and restarts — this form lets the loop follow that.

**When to use:** When forced order is the risk and the work jumps, stalls, and restarts.

**Shape:** Any P can connect to any other. Rupture is allowed; there is no forced centre.

**First steps:**
1. Use the core loop until it is already working for you.
2. Allow jumps between capacities when new evidence warrants it — no forced sequence.
3. Name the capacity you are in after each jump; keep human judgement visible at Pause/Promote.
