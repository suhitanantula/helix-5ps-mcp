# Population (teaser)

Companions are later forms of the same loop — not extra tools, and not five P installs. Same five capacities. Different organisation.

**What it is:** Several diverse trajectories run in parallel. You stay the reward model.

**Why it works:** You need volume and selection. Seed explorers that differ on purpose, then you Pause/Promote.

**When to use:** When you need volume and selection across diverse trajectories.

**Shape:** Several diverse trajectories in parallel; you stay the reward model and Pause/Promote.

**First steps:**
1. Use the core loop until it is already working for you.
2. Seed explorers that differ on purpose (not variations of one answer).
3. You Pause/Promote — advance, hold, combine or stop — against evidence, values and constraints.
