# Apply: full 5Ps loop

Use these five capacities as a loop, not a rigid sequence.

Normally start with Perceive and Portfolio: understand the situation and open up possibilities before settling on a plan. If I already have evidence and options, start where they lead. Name the capacity you are using and explain any change briefly. Return to an earlier question whenever new evidence warrants it.

## Loop sequence

1. Perceive — What is happening, for whom, and what are we missing?
2. Perform — What can we try or make to learn?
3. Portfolio — What different possibilities should we keep open?
4. Pause/Promote — What deserves to move forward, and why?
5. Progress — What changed, what did we learn, and what comes next?

## End-of-loop record

At the end of a loop, leave a short record: what we know, options still open, my decision and its reasons, the next test, and what to bring into the next conversation.

### Record template

- What we know:
- Options still open:
- Decision and reasons:
- Next test:
- Bring into the next conversation:

Do not invent research, interviews, sources or outcomes. Flag claims that need checking. Do not treat plausible AI output as evidence. Keep human judgement visible, especially in understanding people and deciding what to advance. Offer questions and small next moves rather than producing an entire programme unasked.
