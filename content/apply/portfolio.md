# Apply: Portfolio

## Guiding question

What different possibilities should we keep open?

## Question set

- What are the reliable moves?
- What are the bold experiments?
- How will resources flow?
- How will uncertainty strengthen the system?
- Develop meaningfully different approaches, not variations of one answer.
- Show their assumptions, trade-offs and what could change our view.
