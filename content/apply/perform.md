# Apply: Perform

## Guiding question

What can we try or make to learn?

## Question set

- What concrete action should happen first?
- What intent must be preserved?
- Which repeated decisions could be systematised?
- What needs human judgement?
- Design a small, concrete move: a conversation, sketch, prototype or test.
- State what it should teach us.
- Label simulations as simulations; they do not replace real-world evidence.
