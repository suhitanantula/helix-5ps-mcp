# Apply: Pause/Promote

## Guiding question

What deserves to move forward, and why?

## Question set

- What criteria decide what matters?
- What should be promoted?
- What should be paused or killed?
- What judgement risks must you guard against?
- Compare the options against evidence, values and constraints.
- Ask for judgement before choosing. We may advance, hold, combine or stop an option.
