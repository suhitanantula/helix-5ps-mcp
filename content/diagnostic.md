# Which P do I need?

Route a problem to a capacity using the five questions. Normally start with Perceive and Portfolio: understand the situation and open up possibilities before settling on a plan. If you already have evidence and options, start where they lead.

## Perceive

Guiding question: What is happening, for whom, and what are we missing?

Use when the work needs understanding before solution-building — people affected, missing experience, evidence vs assumptions, weak signals.

## Perform

Guiding question: What can we try or make to learn?

Use when insight needs a small concrete move — a conversation, sketch, prototype or test — with clear intent.

## Portfolio

Guiding question: What different possibilities should we keep open?

Use when one answer is too narrow — meaningfully different approaches, trade-offs, anti-fragility, climbing more than one hill.

## Pause/Promote

Guiding question: What deserves to move forward, and why?

Use when options exist and judgement is needed — promote, hold, combine or stop against evidence, values and constraints.

## Progress

Guiding question: What changed, what did we learn, and what comes next?

Use when the loop must leave a record — expectations vs observations, reusable learning, the next Perceive cycle.

## Default

If the situation is unclear, start with Perceive. If the landscape of options is thin, open Portfolio before locking a plan.
